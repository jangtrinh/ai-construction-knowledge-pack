## 1. Executive summary
- **No independent, before/after, peer-reviewed measurement exists for any tech here.** Every "X% less theft" is vendor, operator-reported, or confounded. Treat all effect numbers as direction, not ROI.
- **Best-evidenced measure is non-tech/hybrid, in the UK:** Network Rail cable theft incidents down 67% in a year, cost down from GBP 18.3m (2011/12) to GBP 12.8m. Same source credits police + Scrap Metal Dealers Act + hardened cable, so the tech share is not separable.
- **Machine theft: registration + immobiliser + tracker + insurer rating works as a system (UK CESAR/PANIU/Thatcham).** Theft "down >60%", recovery 28% vs 8% (CEA/OEM claim), ~12.5% premium discount at 3 stars (Met Police commander). Replicable idea: owner-controlled registry + covert tracker + insurer incentive.
- **Corridor intrusion detection by fibre (DAS) is real on pipelines/rail (China 145 km, Germany 36 km)** but needs fibre in the ground. During expressway construction there is none -> DEFER unless the ITS duct is pulled early.
- **Drone-in-a-box: strong response-time numbers only from urban police (Skydio Brookhaven <60 s, vendor-published).** Zero published construction/linear-site security metrics found. BVLOS permission is the gate; Vietnam regime NOT researched.
- **Ground robots (Spot, Knightscope) are not corridor-security tools.** IPVM: rough terrain "would likely neutralize" Knightscope; Spot is used for progress capture. Use at nodes only, if at all.
- **People access is the weakest-evidenced and highest-severity gap.** Nepal: 542 drowned in 3 yrs (59% children) in unguarded construction/excavation pits. Vietnam: children drowned in a pit with "neither warning signs nor fences" (Thanh Hoa); no official statistics. No tech with a measured effect found; physical cover/fence + pit register is the evidence-backed fix.
- **Virtual fence (collars) works for owned livestock (US trial 99% sheep / 96% cattle compliance), not for strangers' cattle or children.** DEFER.
- **Connectivity**: LoRaWAN is cheap but range numbers for solar gateways are vendor claims; satellite-IoT pricing found is dated (2021-22). Both need a site survey/quote.
- **Ranked recommendation (section 6):** (1) machine registry+covert tracker+insurer, (2) cable/material tamper alarms + forensic marking at nodes, (3) pit/people physical controls + register, (4) remote-monitored solar towers w/ thermal at pads + VSOC, (5) drone pilot (after regulatory check), (6) DAS only if fibre exists.

## 3. Case studies (opened sources)
1. **UK Network Rail cable theft programme (UK).** Peak 2011/12 cost GBP 18.3m; 2013 report: incidents -67%, delay minutes halved, cost GBP 12.8m. Measures: SmartWater per-mile codes, GPS log, UV checks at scrap yards, buried cable, CCTV, trembler alarms, train-mounted cameras, police sweeps of scrap dealers. Police and Network Rail both cite the Scrap Metal Dealers Act. Lesson: market-side enforcement beat device-side alone; devices raise detection and evidence. https://www.railtechnologymagazine.com/Rail-News/big-fall-in-cable-theft-over-past-year--network-rail ; https://www.networkrailmediacentre.co.uk/news/campaign-to-stop-thieves-on-the-line
2. **CESAR / PANIU / Thatcham (UK plant).** CEA asked Met Police in 2006 (theft ~GBP 1.5m/week); PANIU formed Oct 2008, funded by insurer donations; theft peaked at ~GBP 2m/week Oct 2010; OEMs encouraged to fit CESAR as standard; Thatcham 5-star rating gives ~12.5% discount at 3 stars. CEA/OEM later claim >60% fewer thefts and 28% vs 8% recovery. Lesson: the lever was industry+police+insurer alignment, not the tag. https://www.pbctoday.co.uk/news/planning-construction-news/combating-machinery-theft/15960/ ; https://hyundai-ce.eu/en/news/2020-01-pr-hyundai-cesar-club
3. **GDNGG 145 km gas pipeline DAS (China).** Two months: 96 alarms, 90% verified on patrol; one excavation stopped above a pipe at 1.5 m. Vendor write-up, no missed-event stats. https://www.worldpipelines.com/equipment-and-safety/17012019/pipeline-intrusion-detection-system-case-study/
4. **Deutsche Bahn DAS trial (Germany).** 2018-19 Berlin, reuses existing trackside fibre, no closures; trial ended 2019; no numbers. https://www.apsensing.com/en/news/case-study-deutsche-bahn-rail-trials
5. **Brookhaven PD Skydio Dock (US, police).** 8 docks, launch on any call, <60 s response claimed; placement and number of docks drive time. Transfer value for the enterprise = dock spacing logic, not the number. https://www.skydio.com/customer-stories/brookhaven-pd-unlocks-30-second-response-times-with-the-deployment-of-8-new-docks
6. **Pits that kill (Nepal + Vietnam).** Madhesh 542 deaths/3 yrs; Thanh Hoa two children, no fence/sign; Vietnam has no official stats and legal responsibility sits on whoever manages the site (a lawyer quoted in Viet Nam News cites Penal Code Art. 285: INFERENCE, not verified law). Lesson: highest-severity people risk on an open corridor is water-filled holes in monsoon. https://kathmandupost.com/province-no-2/2025/05/26/open-pits-in-madhesh-continue-to-swallow-children-in-the-monsoon ; https://news.tuoitre.vn/vietnamese-siblings-drown-after-falling-into-pit-at-utility-pole-construction-site-10359654.htm ; https://vietnamnews.vn/opinion/in-the-spotlight/405230/no-culpability-as-children-drown-in-manholes-sewers.html

## 4. Limits / failure modes
- **Effect numbers are weak.** Confounders (law, copper price, COVID closures), self-reporting, no controls. Never put "-67%" or "-60%" into a the enterprise ROI as a promise.
- **False alarms.** Only vendor figures found (Calipsa "~90%" fewer, Magos "near zero" - snippets). Real corridor with animals, wind, vegetation, heat shimmer: unknown. Budget pilot to measure per-km alarms/night and verified ratio (the one hard metric: GDNGG 90% of alarms verified).
- **Fibre dependency.** DAS needs buried/aerial fibre along the alignment; not there during earthworks.
- **Power/connectivity.** Solar towers and docks need sun + backhaul; LoRa gateway ranges collapse with terrain. Satellite IoT cost/availability unverified.
- **Jamming/tamper.** GPS-only tracker is silent when jammed; require "silence = alarm". Covert tracker + OEM telematics = two independent paths.
- **Drone regulation.** BVLOS needs authorisation (EASA SAIL/SORA for DJI Dock 3; UK CAA approved Network Rail BVLOS 400 m). Vietnam rules not researched. Source: https://enterprise-insights.dji.com/blog/achieve-compliance-operational-success-for-dji-dock-3 (VENDOR).
- **Robot terrain.** Knightscope fails on rough ground (IPVM, 2016); Spot battery ~90 min and IP54 are UNVERIFIED (spec PDF unreadable).
- **Privacy/legal.** Cameras on public road edges, worker GNSS tags, face data: PDPD (Vietnam data protection decree) check needed; not researched.
- **Human response.** Detection without a dispatcher + police agreement = no effect. Night guards on call, police MOU, and scrap-yard checks are part of the system.
- **Vendor bias.** Rows 1, 3-5, 10, 13-16 are vendor-sourced.

## 6. Ranked recommendation for the enterprise
1. **Machine: registry + covert tracker + insurer deal (MODIFY, N+C).** Highest evidence chain (CESAR/PANIU/Thatcham), cheapest, builds on ISO 15143-3. Get a written insurer quote first.
2. **Material: tamper alarm + forensic marking at nodes; cable-drum/rebar counts at gate (MODIFY/NEW, N).** Evidence = Network Rail bundle. Cost low. Add scrap-yard outreach with provincial police.
3. **People: pit/pond register + physical cover/fence + flashing sign + monsoon backfill rule (NEW, C).** Evidence is severity, not tech; cheapest life-safety item. Tech alert (camera/LoRa) is optional layer after.
4. **VSOC with remote verification + talk-down for solar towers at pads (MODIFY, N+pads).** Use RVM cost logic (guards 25-40 USD/h benchmark in US; VN wage differs, so recompute). Pilot to measure alarms/km/night.
5. **Fuel: tank-approach sensor + level monitor on bowsers/depots (MODIFY, N+C).** Cheap; measure baseline loss first.
6. **Drone dock pilot (DEFER until VN BVLOS rule known).** One dock at one pad; success metric = launch-to-eyes time and verified-alarm share.
7. **DAS (DEFER)** unless duct/fibre laid early. **Virtual fence, ground robots, satellite IoT (DEFER).**
