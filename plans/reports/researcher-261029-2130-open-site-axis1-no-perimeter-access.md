## 1. Executive summary

- Closed-site logic = "check identity ONCE at ONE choke point". On a corridor there is no choke point. World practice moves the check to 4 places: (a) one accountable person per work group, (b) a virtual boundary (geofence), (c) the machine, (d) natural pinch points (tunnel portals, bridge/pier pads, possession access points).
- Best-documented corridor model = Network Rail UK: Sentinel card (central competence + medical register) + COSS who stays with the group from entry to exit, checks each person's Sentinel record, sets protection. Identity+competence+accountability = a PERSON and a REGISTER, not a gate. [FACT]
- Network Rail sentinel "swipe" is done by the COSS before each shift (not at a gate). [FACT, 2020 post]
- Geofence + QR at site boundary is mainstream for short-term/linear-ish sites: NSW Gov rail sector, >1,000 short-term worksites/yr, induction 10–15 min -> <1 min (vendor case, Sine). [FACT, vendor-sourced]
- Wearable presence on corridors works for GNSS, not for crowdsourced BLE: MoDOT/Univ. Missouri 2026 — GNSS "more stable", costs battery/cost/data-access; BLE crowdsourced "large location errors, especially rural". [FACT]
- UWB / BLE-anchor / camera Re-ID (TP1.5) only make sense at NODES and pinch points. Morley Tunnel (UK rail): tag + receiver at each portal -> live "who is inside" for ~150 staff/shift; proven in a real evacuation. [FACT]
- Lone-worker/man-down is a NEW corridor requirement with no closed-site equivalent: no-movement alert after 180 s to a 24/7 centre (Tarmac armband), check-in timers that tolerate signal loss (Heartbeat). [FACT; both older sources]
- Public traffic intrusion is a NEW corridor hazard; closed sites have none. Amey/National Highways A52: cone lasers + CCTV + wireless alarm button replaced staffing every access point. TTI closed-course: worn alarms 97% (misses = out of comms range). NASEM 2025: intrusion alerts "mixed results", mobile work zones a feasibility concern. [FACT]
- HK made 4S (digital tracking, e-access control, smart worker devices) mandatory >HK$30m since Feb 2023; govt reports industry accident rate down ~20% 2023–2025. This is correlation across the whole industry, NOT proof tracking caused it. [FACT]
- Not found: any Vietnamese highway workforce-tracking example (only traffic AI cameras), Singapore LTA, Japan MLIT, HS2, Kwant, Hilti worker wearables. See §4/§6. Do not assume they exist.
- Vietnam legal constraint is live: Decree 356/2025/ND-CP (effective 2026-01-01) — location and biometric data carry extra breach-notice duty to the data subject within 72h. Face ID (TP1.1) and any gait vector (TP1.5) need a data-protection review before scale-out. [FACT via EY; gait-as-biometric = INFERENCE]

## 3. Notable case studies

1. **Network Rail UK — Sentinel + COSS + possession protection.** Identity, competence and accountability are separated from any gate: central card register, COSS stays with the group, checks each member, positions lookouts; Protection Controller owns a shared line blockage. Best conceptual template for the enterprise "work-front controller". Caveat: sources are a 2020 company post and a third-party study guide; rail rules are stricter than highway.
   https://info.railsentinel.co.uk/2020/04/14/swipe-for-your-safety-with-sentinel/ ; https://open-exam-prep.com/study-guides/uk-pts-track-safety/safe-systems-of-work-and-safety-roles/trackside-safety-roles
2. **Morley Tunnel (TRU West Alliance, UK).** 4-week blockade, ~150 staff/shift; tags + receivers at tunnel ends -> live in/out list; used in a real fire evacuation. Shows the "pinch point" pattern: use a physical choke where one exists.
   https://ccsbestpractice.org.uk/?p=94849
3. **A52 Nottinghamshire (Amey for National Highways), March 2024, 15 nights.** Cone lasers + CCTV + wireless alarm button instead of staffing every access point; resident access managed. Mobile "gate" = sensing + alarm.
   https://www.highwaysmagazine.co.uk/news/strategic-road-network/hi-tech-warning-protect-workers-a52
4. **Tarmac National Contracting (UK) lone-worker armband.** Satellite-positioned armband, 180 s no-motion -> vibrate -> 24/7 centre; panic alarm records audio. Old (~2013) but exact mechanism documented.
   https://www.ciht.org.uk/news/lone-workers-to-be-tracked-for-safety-by-lafarge-tarmac/
5. **Hong Kong 4S.** Mandatory for capital works >HK$30m since Feb 2023; govt (LegCo reply, FACT) states overall construction accident rate fell ~20% 2023–2025. Adoption figures differ by source: ~60% of >HK$30m projects per OpenGov; ~80% of sites per RTHK (SNIPPET, different populations). Strongest Asian regulatory-mandate precedent; mostly building sites, no linear-works case found.
   https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202606/24/P2026062400525p.htm ; https://www.digitalpolicy.gov.hk/en/our_work/success_stories/smart_site_safety_system/
6. **Nanchang Railway Bureau (China) BeiDou e-fence for track work.** RTK positioning + dynamic geofence: alerts on early entry, late exit, out-of-zone work. Still in data-collection stage (Oct 2026); closest analogue to a "digital possession". Not production evidence.
   https://www.stdaily.com/web/gdxw/2026-10/12/content_594720.html

## 4. Limits / failure modes

- **GNSS under bridges / urban canyon.** SNIPPET only: open-sky 2–3 m, urban canyon 10 m to >30 m (geo-matching.com summary). No fetched source specific to bridge decks or underpasses. INFERENCE: tags will drop or drift under spans and in cuttings; design fallback (last-known + pinch-point counts).
  https://geo-matching.com/articles/why-does-gnss-positioning-drift-in-urban-canyons
- **Battery / cost / data access of GNSS tags.** FACT (MoDOT 2026). Nanchang claims 3 days -> 1 year terminal battery (claim, unmeasured).
- **Crowd-BLE is unreliable in rural areas.** FACT (MoDOT 2026) — kills a cheap "just BLE tag + passing phones" design on rural highway sections.
- **Worn intrusion alarms have a range limit.** FACT (TTI closed-course, 2018; closed-course, not live work).
- **Intrusion tech maturity.** FACT (NASEM 2025): mixed results, no cost-effectiveness data, mobile work zones feasibility concern.
- **Geofence check-in weak spots.** SNIPPET: GPS weakest in the very places work happens; cell-dependent apps fail; GPS spoofable (MISA).
- **Fixed-site wearables don't transfer.** Triax Spot-r runs on a project-specific network with gateways, marketed for vertical/multi-storey sites (SNIPPET/vendor). Needs re-design for km-long corridors.
- **Privacy / labour.** FACT: Decree 356/2025/ND-CP (EY): location and biometric data -> extra 72 h data-subject breach notice; employee-data policy and consent forms required. FACT (RailTech 2014): UK lone-worker service is opt-in, activated per use, and employers can reach worker data only by court order — a design precedent for limiting always-on tracking to the shift. Whether always-on location tracking of a worker is lawful under Vietnamese Labour Code: NOT researched.
- **Evidence quality.** Sine = vendor case study; Triax = vendor; open-exam-prep = third-party guide; Sentinel post 2020, RailTech 2014, Tarmac ~2013 (old); gov.uk "game changer" release was withdrawn 2017 and is dropped; HK accident-drop is industry-wide correlation; TTI is closed-course.
- **Process limit.** Controller-based identity (rows 2, 16) depends on a competent, honest controller; Network Rail layers a central register, lookouts and PC on top. A single foreman scanning IDs is a weaker control.
- **Targets searched, not found (do not assume they exist):** Kwant (no hit), Hilti worker wearables (only ON!Track asset tags surfaced), Singapore LTA, Japan MLIT i-Construction worker tracking (only Kajima/Obayashi heat-stroke wearables via SNIPPET), HS2, any Vietnamese highway workforce-tracking project (only traffic AI cameras on operating expressways), Australia highway (only NSW rail / Austroads project pages, no tracking result).
