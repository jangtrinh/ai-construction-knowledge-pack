# Axis 4/4 — Corridor infrastructure, logistics & Vietnam context (CC1 open sites)

Date 2026-10-29. Evidence tags: **FACT** = page/PDF opened this session (URL given). **VENDOR** = fact about a vendor claim, not independent. **INFERENCE** = my reasoning. **UNVERIFIED** = seen only in a search digest or not opened. No Vietnamese legal number below comes from memory; every row in Table B states what was opened and how.

## 1. Executive summary

1. Closed-site "Core infra" (fibre + one edge box + grid power + one gate) does not survive on a corridor. Keep it at NODES (casting yard, batching plant, camp, depot, pier pads). The corridor needs: solar/genset power per unit, cellular-first + offline-first software, LEO backup, geofence/e-ticket in place of a gate. (INFERENCE from the facts below)
2. Strongest evidence is for **e-Ticketing**: FHWA/state DOTs name ticket-taker safety as the primary driver; Georgia DOT: ~1 h saved per paving day, up to 15,000 tickets per asphalt job, mandatory since 2021. Dead zones at remote plants/quarries are the documented weak point; DOTs use offline mode, signal boosters, QR on paper tickets, LEO. (FACT, FHWA)
3. **Weighbridge + LiDAR at the gate does not translate** to many drop points. Replace with weigh-at-source (plant weighbridge at the NODE) + e-ticket + geofence time-stamps at the drop. Payload-on-truck is a vendor spec only (UNVERIFIED).
4. Mobile solar surveillance (LVT-type) is real tech (2x400 W panels + battery + genset, cellular with satellite option) but **all published effects are vendor claims, none from road projects**. Use for laydown yards/pier pads/parked plant, not as a corridor-wide system.
5. Equipment theft is a real, measured cost in the US: 11,493 thefts reported, 22% recovered, ~USD 300M (2015, NER). The "90%+ recovery with GPS" figure is vendor marketing — do not put in the proposal.
6. Fleet data: ISO 15143-3 (AEMP 2.0) gives a common JSON/XML feed (5 core fields: position, hours, fuel total, fuel 24h, distance; optional incl. payload counts) but OEM differences persist — plan for per-OEM adapters + aftermarket GPS/fuel sensors for old/Chinese/rental plant. No measured productivity gain from haul-cycle tracking found in peer-reviewed/agency sources.
7. Vietnam connectivity: 99.8% of **population** covered by 3G/4G/5G (Apr 2025) — says nothing about corridor km. Starlink is **commercial since 13 Aug 2026** (4 gateways, cap 600k terminals, kit VND 8.7-10.3M, residential VND 1.1-1.7M/month, business = usage-billed, no published terms for vehicle-mounted use). Terminal draw 25-100 W — solar-feasible. (FACT)
8. CC1 packages are 10-40 km linear JV sections with heavy bridge content (XL03 Hau Giang-Ca Mau: 12 km main line, **24 road bridges**, 34 months from Jan 2023) — a string of bridge-pier NODES along a CORRIDOR. XL03 is the **reference pattern**, not the pilot: its contract term has probably ended (completion status UNVERIFIED). Live candidates with evidence: Cat Lai and Long Hung bridges (PPP, groundbreaking planned 15 Jan 2026), Vanh dai 4 component 3 (CC1 49% of investor vehicle), North-South East phase 2 "transition packages" (named in a 15 Sep 2025 article, no detail).
9. Regulatory hooks that change the design: operating-road works need a permit + traffic-safety measures + public info board + notification (TT 41/2024/TT-BGTVT as amended by TT 72/2025/TT-BXD on 31/12/2025: permit valid 24 months, 5 working days to decide, issuers Khu Quan ly duong bo / So Xay dung / UBND cap xa); drone flights need MoD flight permit and licensed pilots (ND 288/2025/NĐ-CP, from 5 Nov 2025); personal-data law 91/2025/QH15 + ND 356/2025/NĐ-CP in force since 1 Jan 2026 (affects Face ID/worker tracking); BIM mandatory for grade I+ public/PPP works from 2023, grade II+ from 2025 (QĐ 258/QĐ-TTg).
10. Biggest gap: no source found showing a road contractor running an edge compute node that moves with the work front, nor any LVT-type unit on a road build. Treat both as design bets, not proven practice.

## 5. Limits / failure modes

- **US/EU-centric evidence.** E-ticketing and theft numbers are US; VN has no equivalent published data (no VN theft statistic, no VN e-ticketing pilot found).
- **Vendor-heavy for security/fuel/telematics.** LVT, Technoton, AutoPi, Trimble claims are not independent; no number from them should be quoted as "measured effect" without the VENDOR tag.
- **Wrong-dimension risk.** 99.8% 4G is population coverage. Real km coverage on CC1's Mekong/central packages is unknown; do a drive test before pricing connectivity.
- **Starlink for mobile machines** is untested legally; only fixed pads are safe to plan.
- **Law currency risk.** Rules move fast: TT 41/2024 was already amended on 31/12/2025 (permit issuers and timing changed; confirmed in the consolidated text). Other instruments (a draft QCVN 41 revision, a newer PDP penalty decree) appear only in search snippets, were not fetched, and are in the UNVERIFIED ledger. Re-check before any compliance claim.
- **thuvienphapluat.vn / vbpl.vn blocked** — all Vietnamese texts are mirrors/secondary; none are the Công báo original.
- **Edge-moves-with-front** and **LVT-on-roads** have no direct evidence; design bets.
- **Traffic-signage detail** (cones, tapers, speed limits in work zones) not retrieved; the QCVN 41:2024 work-zone content must be read from the full text.
